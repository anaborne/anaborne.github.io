Football squares listed as contracts: 100 boxes at the half and 100 at the final. A box pays $1 if the last digit of each team's score matches. Prices below are fair values for this week's games, read from the spread and total that Kalshi's books imply.

Where the prices come from

Each team's digit odds are a weighted count over past team-games with a similar implied team total. The two teams are multiplied and corrected for the pairs that happen together more often than chance, such as 0-0 after one quarter. Prices carry no maker spread.
